IntelliHire

Overview
IntelliHire is an AI-powered interview preparation platform developed as a Final Year Project (FYP). The platform provides a realistic mock interview experience using Agentic AI, enabling users to practice interviews tailored to specific companies, roles, and domains. By combining Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing (NLP), IntelliHire delivers intelligent, personalized, and context-aware interview sessions.

Users can upload resumes, job descriptions, company documents, or preparation material, allowing the AI to generate interview questions and feedback based on their own content.

Features

Upload resumes, job descriptions, and preparation documents (PDFs, CVs, etc.)

How It Works
Users create an account and select an interview type or target company.
Users can upload resumes, job descriptions, or interview preparation documents.
The RAG pipeline retrieves relevant information from uploaded documents to provide context for the interview.
Agentic AI coordinates multiple specialized AI agents to conduct a personalized interview session.
The system evaluates verbal responses, communication quality, and facial expressions.
After the interview, users receive detailed feedback, performance scores, and personalized recommendations for improvement.
